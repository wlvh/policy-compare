Trace every change to its source.

This pilot invites 5–10 people to try the tool on their own networks and computers, using their usual review process. The fixed candidate is __RELEASE_ID__, powered by MuPDF and a Rust WebAssembly comparison core.

Start with the fictional samples built into the tool. Before using your own documents, confirm that you may process them on this computer. The tool compares extractable text and does not perform OCR. Unresolved matches and content that has not been compared are reported separately.

Open this candidate

Your PDFs are processed in your browser. This site serves application files; it has no document upload or feedback service and adds no analytics scripts. The hosting provider still receives the connection information needed to deliver web resources. The exported comparison JSON contains source text: check your permission to share it. “Download diagnostics” includes only version, engine, browser, processing stage and error codes.

Report a problem

Record what you did, what you expected and what happened, then send the report through the channel provided by your inviter. For comparison errors, also include the sample ID and page numbers in both documents. Do not publicly post results, screenshots or files containing business document text.
